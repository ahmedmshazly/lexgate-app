## Greenfield/no-migration declaration

**Proposed position:** Greenfield/no migration. No historical client, prospect, party/contact, matter, document/evidence, finance, or user/access record is imported. No manual re-keying is permitted to bypass the migration gate. Only local synthetic fixtures may be used.

Any deviation requires the separate [Migration Decision Pack](odr-004-migration-decision-pack.md), Privacy/DPO and Security/Engineering review where applicable, lawyer review of conflict/engagement implications, and recorded Product/legal-operations approval.

## Approval record

- **Product lead:** No approval recorded
- **Legal-operations lead:** No approval recorded
- **Required sign-off:** completed topics, evidence links, open decisions, residual risk, change triggers, exact pilot scope, reviewer/date, expiry/review date, and release impact.
- **Safe interim behavior:** no firm operating assumption, source system, taxonomy, volume, channel, or migration is activated.
