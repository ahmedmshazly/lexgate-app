# ODR-003 Per-workflow Processing Review

**Status:** Controlled review template — blocked  
**Accountable reviewer:** Privacy/DPO reviewer with Egyptian legal support  
**Classification:** Internal; synthetic example only  
**Decision rule:** No real personal data, privacy notice, consent capture, provider, processor, transfer, or production workflow is authorized by this template. **No approval recorded.**

## Reviewer instructions and authority

Complete one record for each proposed workflow before it is enabled. This packet is an engineering intake record, not Egyptian legal advice and not a determination that Lex Gate, a firm, or a person is a data user, controller, processor, or DPO-appointer.

The reviewer must use the controlling Arabic legal source and approved legal interpretation. Current primary reference material is the PDPC’s [DPO guidance](https://pdpc.gov.eg/assets/pdf-data/Guidelines/DPO.pdf), version 1.1 dated 26 January 2026, which identifies Law No. 151 of 2020 and Executive Regulations Decision No. 816 of 2025 as controlling. PDPC guidance may change and does not replace the law or qualified interpretation.

## Privacy notice and consent separation

### Privacy notice

A Privacy notice explains an approved workflow in the form and language determined by the reviewer. It is not a consent mechanism and must not be silently represented as one. Record the required audience, language/Arabic presentation, accessibility, delivery timing, content owner, version, and proof of publication only after approval.

### Consent

Consent is not the default lawful basis. Where the reviewer identifies Consent as necessary, record its precise purpose, requested act, evidence of the relevant standard, withdrawal route, effect of withdrawal, version, language/accessibility, and whether service remains available without it. A generic checkbox, bundled statement, or pre-ticked control is not accepted by this engineering template.

### Synthetic example only

PRV-SYN-001 illustrates the review shape: synthetic Arabic/English training fixture; no person; local repository only; no sharing; retention limited to development fixture lifecycle. It is not a model lawful-basis decision for a client, employee, prospect, or any real person.

## Required evidence before any future activation

- A completed workflow record with links to the controlling source and approved legal interpretation.
- DPO determination and, where applicable, appointment/registration evidence in the companion control review.
- Approved notice and any separate consent mechanism, with language and accessibility review.
- Approved data inventory, processor/transfer posture, retention/hold route, rights route, security/risk assessment, and breach route.
- A privacy/DPO reviewer outcome that identifies exact scope, owner, expiry/review date, residual risk, and release impact.

Until then, local synthetic fixtures are the only permitted data.
