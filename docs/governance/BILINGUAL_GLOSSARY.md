# Bilingual Legal and Product Glossary

**Document ID:** LG-GOV-004  
**Status:** Proposed; legal terms require reviewer confirmation

| Controlled English     | Arabic reference       | Engineering meaning                                                             |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Client                 | موكّل                  | A party with an accepted client relationship; not merely an enquiry.            |
| Prospective client     | عميل محتمل             | A person or organization under intake/conflict consideration.                   |
| Matter                 | ملف / موضوع قانوني     | Controlled unit of legal work; distinct from engagement and proceeding.         |
| Proceeding             | دعوى / إجراء قضائي     | Court, administrative, arbitration, or other procedural stream within a matter. |
| Engagement             | اتفاق / تكليف          | Accepted representation or service arrangement.                                 |
| Deadline               | ميعاد قانوني           | A legally material deadline with source, rule version, and confirmation state.  |
| Evidence               | دليل / مستند إثبات     | A separately classified evidentiary item; not every document is evidence.       |
| Legal hold             | وقف الإتلاف القانوني   | A suspension of normal destruction pending a legal or investigative reason.     |
| Restricted matter      | ملف مقيّد              | Matter requiring additional access controls.                                    |
| Professional authority | الصفة / السلطة المهنية | Legally or professionally permitted authority, separate from system role.       |

Arabic primary legal texts control where applicable. This glossary is not a substitute for source-specific legal interpretation.
