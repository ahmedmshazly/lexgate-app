# Firm Discovery and User Research Protocol

**Document ID:** LG-PRD-002  
**Status:** Blocked pending field evidence  
**Owner:** Product / legal operations lead

## Purpose

Capture how Lex Gate really operates before permanently configuring practice areas, matter taxonomy, authority, communications, finance, migration, and retention behavior. Generic foreign law-firm patterns are not evidence.

## Required evidence

1. Legal entity, registrations, signatories, offices, practice areas, team structure, and professional credentials.
2. Intake, conflict, consultation, engagement, matter-opening, deadline, hearing, document, communication, billing, closure, and complaint workflows.
3. Current tools, file locations, physical records, interfaces, access groups, data volumes, and migration quality.
4. Representative Arabic/English user journeys, accessibility needs, channel preferences, and exception cases.

## Method

Use structured interviews, observed workflow walkthroughs, redacted sample artifacts, role/playback sessions, and usability tests. Record source, participant role, confidence, date, and affected requirement IDs. Never upload client matter data to this repository.

## Conversion rule

Every finding becomes one of: confirmed fact, validated need, proposed design, rejected assumption, risk, or open decision. A finding does not change canonical requirements until its accountable owner reviews it.
