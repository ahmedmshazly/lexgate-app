# Product Brief and Scope

**Document ID:** LG-PRD-001  
**Status:** Proposed future-state baseline  
**Owner:** Project Sponsor

## Vision

Lex Gate gives clients a clear, secure engagement experience and gives legal professionals reliable operational context without compromising professional judgment, confidentiality, legal deadlines, evidentiary integrity, privacy, or service quality.

## Outcomes

- Centralize institutional records without granting indiscriminate file access.
- Make conflict, engagement, responsibility, deadlines, records, and communications visible and auditable.
- Support Arabic/English work and client communication without reducing Arabic legal meaning to a translation afterthought.
- Improve service reliability without presenting automation as legal judgment.

## User groups

Prospective client, client representative, client user, lawyer, supervising lawyer, legal assistant, operations staff, finance staff, records/privacy staff, security/technical administrator, and external provider. A job title never decides access or professional authority by itself.

## Scope discipline

The future product includes intake through closure, public content, internal practice operations, client collaboration, finance, records, privacy, reporting, and approved integrations. It does not try to replace professional judgment, a court system, a tax adviser, or a regulator.

## Success measures

Measure authenticated completion of controlled workflows, timely ownership and escalation, traceable records, denial of unauthorized access, bilingual usability, and recovery readiness. Do not optimize for raw automation volume, client-data collection, unrestricted visibility, or superficial “green” test counts.
